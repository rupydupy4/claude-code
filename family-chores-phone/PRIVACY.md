# Family Chores: privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026. The same policy is shown inside the app (Settings → Privacy policy,
or the "Privacy" link on the first screen) and online at
https://rupydupy4.github.io/claude-code/family-chores/#/privacy

## The short version

Family Chores collects no data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no adverts and no
tracking. Everything you put in the app stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

- Your household name, first names, chores, rewards, points and settings are saved in the app's own
  storage on your device.
- Photos a child adds as proof are resized and saved in the app's own storage on your device.
- The parent PIN is never stored as typed: only a salted, one-way fingerprint (hash) of it is kept.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. If you use your phone's own system backup
(for example iCloud Backup on an iPhone), the app's storage may be included in it, as it is for other
apps; that backup belongs to you and the developer has no access to it. Android's automatic app
backup is switched off for Family Chores.

## Camera and photos

The app uses the camera or photo library only when a child chooses to add a photo to a chore. The
photo is kept on the device and never uploaded. On Android, "Take a photo" hands over to the phone's
camera app, so Family Chores itself asks for no camera permission.

## Backups you make

A parent can save a backup file from Settings. It contains the household's data and photos. It is
only created when you ask, and you choose where it goes (Files, a cloud drive, email…). Treat it like
any private family document.

## Children

The app is designed for families and is set up and controlled by a parent, behind a parent PIN. It
collects no personal information from children, has no chat or contact with other people, no
adverts, no links out for children and no in-app purchases.

## Deleting your data

A parent can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also
removes it. Because the developer holds no copy, data can't be recovered after deletion unless you
saved a backup.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and here with a new date.
